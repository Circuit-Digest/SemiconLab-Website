OUR STORY

Building Platforms that Power Your Next Big Idea

Semicon Lab (SemiconLab) is an open-hardware initiative dedicated to building practical, scalable, and application-specific platforms for the electronics engineering community across India and the globe.

We design integrated hardware and software platforms that make technology adoption easier, faster, and more accessible. Backed by the trusted documentation culture, real-world experience, and community feedback from CircuitDigest.

To become a community-trusted open hardware brand that enables engineers, makers, and innovators to rapidly adapt, experiment, and develop technology, with a strong focus on India's growing engineering ecosystem.

We aim to empower the electronics community with hardware, software, and learning resources that reduce barriers to entry and accelerate innovation.

Semicon Lab is an initiative by Semicon Media, born from a decade of experience in electronics design and community building. After inspiring millions to build electronics, we recognized the need for a mature, local open hardware ecosystem in India.

Our mission is to bridge this gap, enabling engineers, designers, and startups to build and scale their ideas faster using open, well-documented platforms.

Our Products

Application-Specific Hardware Ecosystems

Each product family includes open-source hardware, firmware, libraries, tutorials, and community support. Everything you need to build.

LiteWing ESP32 Drone

Open-source ESP32 drone that flies out of the box. Control via smartphone or Python code. Modular, beginner-friendly platform for learning and development.

Flight Stabilizer Module

Add-on shield for LiteWing and ESP32 drones with ToF and optical flow sensors. Enables height hold and position hold with easy setup and detailed tutorials.

GL868-DEV Cellular IoT Board with GPS

Low-cost cellular IoT and GPS module based on SIM868. Onboard microcontroller and antenna for quick deployment and rapid development.

GL868-ESP32 Cellular IoT Board (GSM+GPS+WiFi+BLE)

ESP32 + SIM868 combo board for IoT and GPS tracking. Includes Airtel SIM and 18650 battery for rapid development and deployment.

GL868-MOD GSM/GPS Board with Antenna

SIM868-based 2G module with onboard antenna and Airtel SIM. Breadboard-friendly breakout for quick prototyping and testing.

ESP32 Smart Glass

Modular ESP32 smart glass with camera, mic, and speaker. Supports TTS, STT, and image recognition for customizable solutions.
